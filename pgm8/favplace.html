MY FAVOURITE PLACE


Munnar

Munnar is a beautiful hill station in Kerala, India. It is famous for its green tea gardens, high mountains, and cool weather. Many people visit to enjoy the peaceful views, scenic waterfalls, and fresh air.

Nestled in the Western Ghats of Kerala, Munnar is a serene hill station renowned for its rolling tea plantations, mist-shrouded valleys, and rich biodiversity. Situated at an elevation of roughly 1,600 meters above sea level, this former summer resort of the British Raj offers a refreshing escape with its cool climate, cascading waterfalls, and winding mountain roads. Home to the endangered Nilgiri Tahr at Eravikulam National Park and Anamudi—the highest peak in South India—Munnar seamlessly combines tranquil natural beauty with outdoor adventure, making it a premier destination for trekkers, nature lovers, and peaceful getaways alike.
